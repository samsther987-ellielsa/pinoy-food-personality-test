How Our Food Quiz Works

Our food personality quiz is an independent entertainment quiz. It uses four pairs of letters familiar from MBTI: E/I, S/N, T/F and J/P. It is not the official MBTI assessment and is not affiliated with its provider. The result is a playful prompt, not a psychological diagnosis or a recommendation about what you should eat.

What the score means

Each run chooses four questions from each of four groups, for sixteen questions in total. Each answer contributes one or three points to one side of its group. Some statements reverse that scoring direction. The side with the higher total supplies the letter. A tie selects E, S, T or J. This rule makes a result possible; it does not mean those letters are objectively more accurate.

Question selection is randomized. Different questions or answers can produce a different result. Going back removes the last answer before you choose again. Your quiz answers are processed locally; the result and date are saved in your browser when storage is available.

How the pairings work

The letter-to-dish table below is fixed. It is our creative mapping, not a measured relationship between personality and food. Sinigang maps to ISFJ and champorado to INFP. Pop-culture names shown with a result are also playful comparisons; they do not verify an artist’s or character’s MBTI. Choose food using ingredients, taste, allergies and cooking needs.

Ano ang ibig sabihin ng score

May apat na tanong mula sa bawat isa sa apat na grupo, kaya labing-anim lahat. Nagbibigay ang sagot ng isa o tatlong puntos sa isang panig. Baligtad ang direksiyon ng score sa ilang tanong. Ang mas mataas na kabuuan ang nagbibigay ng letra. Kapag tabla, E, S, T o J ang pinipili ng code. Tuntunin ito para makapagbigay ng resulta, hindi patunay ng mas tamang personalidad.

Random ang pagpili ng tanong. Maaaring magbago ang resulta kapag iba ang tanong o sagot. Inaalis ng back button ang huling sagot bago sumagot muli. Lokal ang pagproseso at naka-save sa browser ang resulta at petsa kung may storage.

Paano ginagawa ang mga pares

Nakatakda ang table sa ibaba. Malikhaing pares namin ito, hindi nasukat na ugnayan ng personalidad at pagkain. ISFJ ang sinigang at INFP ang champorado. Pang-aliw din ang pop-culture comparison; hindi nito kinukumpirma ang MBTI ng artist o character. Pumili ng pagkain ayon sa sangkap, panlasa, allergy at kailangan sa pagluto.
