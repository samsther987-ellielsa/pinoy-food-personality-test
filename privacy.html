Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026. This policy describes the current website, including the optional analytics choice. The site is maintained by Sam. For a privacy request, use the contact form or email samsther987@gmail.com.

Quiz answers and browser storage

Quiz answers and scores are processed in your browser; the quiz code does not submit them to our server. Your latest four-letter result and test date are saved in localStorage under lastMBTI and lastTestDate. The analytics choice is saved under pinoy-analytics-consent. These values stay in that browser until changed or cleared. Theme and language changes are temporary page settings. Clearing this site’s browser data removes stored choices and results.

Optional Google Analytics

Analytics is off until you choose “Allow analytics” under Privacy choices at the bottom of a page. With permission, we load Google Analytics (GA4) to understand page visits. It can process page URLs, referring pages, browser/device information and identifiers in analytics cookies. Quiz answers, names and contact-form messages are not sent by our analytics code. Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled in our configuration.

You can choose “Keep analytics off” at any time. This saves the new preference, disables subsequent measurement and removes accessible first-party GA cookies. It cannot retract data already sent to Google. See Google’s privacy policy for Google’s processing. The recipes and quiz work without analytics.

Advertising and external requests

Advertising scripts are currently disabled. Our AdSense publisher verification meta tag and ads.txt identify the publisher; they do not load advertising code. Before advertising is enabled, this policy and any required advertising consent controls will need to match that configuration.

Vercel serves this site and may process request information such as IP address, device information and URLs for hosting and security. The site uses Google Fonts, so font requests go to Google. Some quiz pages request sound effects from Mixkit when their audio is used. Those providers receive the network information needed to deliver the resource. See Vercel’s privacy notice and Mixkit’s privacy policy. External links send you to the destination’s own service when you follow them.

Contact messages

Submitting the contact form sends your name, email and message to Formspree so the site maintainer can receive and respond to it. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request. See Formspree’s privacy policy. Emailing directly uses your email provider.

Retention and privacy requests

Browser storage lasts until you clear it or the site updates the value. Hosting, analytics and form records are subject to the providers’ settings and retention arrangements. We do not claim a specific account retention period that has not been verified. To ask about a message you sent, or request access, correction or deletion of information the maintainer holds, contact Sam and identify the relevant message. Available rights depend on your location. You may also contact your local privacy authority.

Children and policy changes

This site is not intended to collect contact information from children under thirteen. A parent or guardian can contact Sam about a message submitted by a child. This page’s update date will change when the data practices described here change.

Opsyonal na Google Analytics

Hindi gumagana ang analytics hangga’t hindi pinipili ang “Payagan ang analytics” sa Privacy choices sa ibaba. Kapag pinayagan, nilo-load ang Google Analytics (GA4) para maunawaan ang pagbisita. Maaari nitong iproseso ang URL, pinanggalingang pahina, browser/device at identifier sa cookies. Hindi ipinapadala ng analytics code ang sagot sa quiz, pangalan o mensahe sa form. Naka-disable sa configuration ang Google Signals at advertising personalization.

Maaaring piliin ang “Huwag payagan ang analytics” anumang oras. Ise-save ang pagpili, idi-disable ang susunod na pagsukat at buburahin ang naa-access na first-party GA cookies. Hindi nito mababawi ang naipadala na. Basahin ang Google privacy policy. Gagana ang mga recipe at quiz kahit walang analytics.
